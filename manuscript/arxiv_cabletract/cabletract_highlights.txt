Cable-driven field robot keeps heavy bodies off-field; a ~250 kg carriage tills.
Implement co-design cuts median draft ~63%, closing the energy and anchor budgets.
~4x less drawbar energy and ~98% less compacted area than an 80 hp diesel tractor.
NPV-positive as a tractor replacement; off-grid feasibility is climate-dependent.
Five higher-fidelity models bound anchor, depth-control and cable-safety risks.
